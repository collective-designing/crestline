For Suppliers

The process of working with Crestline as a supplier.

We work with selected suppliers through a structured process designed to understand your products, assess market readiness, and prepare a professional path toward Canadian buyers.

01

Submit Your Application

Tell us about your company, product category, production capacity, pricing, packaging, and current export readiness.

02

Product Review

Our team reviews your product quality, market fit, presentation, supplier reliability, and potential demand in Canada.

03

Market Preparation

If selected, we help organize the information buyers need, including product details, wholesale pricing, catalogs, and positioning.

04

Buyer Introduction

Once your product is ready, Crestline works to connect it with suitable Canadian buyers, distributors, or business opportunities.

Not every supplier will be accepted immediately. Crestline focuses on suppliers who are serious, responsive, and ready to build long-term business relationships in Canada.
For Buyers

The process of sourcing products through Crestline.

We help Canadian buyers explore international products through a more organized sourcing process, reducing the uncertainty that often comes with finding suppliers overseas.

01

Share Your Buying Needs

Tell us what product categories, quantities, price ranges, quality expectations, and delivery timelines your business is looking for.

02

Supplier Matching

We review our supplier network and identify products or manufacturers that may be suitable for your business needs.

03

Product & Pricing Review

We help organize product details, pricing, samples, packaging information, and supplier communication so you can review clearly.

04

Order & Relationship Support

Crestline supports the next steps toward orders, repeat supply, logistics discussions, and long-term sourcing relationships.

Our goal is to give Canadian buyers access to promising international products while keeping the process professional, practical, and relationship-focused.
About Crestline Trade Group

A Canadian trade partner connecting global suppliers with real market opportunity.

Crestline Trade Group is a Canadian-based B2B trading and distribution company built to connect capable international suppliers with buyers and business opportunities in Canada.

Our focus is simple: identify quality products, work with serious suppliers, and build reliable pathways for those products to enter the Canadian market through professional sourcing, trading, and distribution support.

We work closely with suppliers who are ready to grow beyond their local market and buyers who need dependable access to competitive, well-presented products.
